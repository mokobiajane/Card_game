Usage Scenario\+:


\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Game Start
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game is initialized, and players are prompted to choose their characters and build their decks from available cards.
\item The initial game board is set up with players and enemies placed in their starting positions.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item Turn Sequence
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Each player takes turns drawing a set number of cards from their deck into their hand.
\item Players use action points to play cards from their hand, such as attacking enemies, casting spells, or using items.
\item \doxylink{classEnemy}{Enemy} characters take their turns to attack players or perform actions based on predefined behaviors.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item Combat Resolution
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The effects of played cards are resolved in a specific order, calculating damage dealt, healing, and status effects applied.
\item Players and enemies update their health and other attributes based on the outcomes of combat.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item End Turn
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Players end their turn by discarding excess cards and preparing for the next turn.
\item Special events or conditions may trigger at the end of the turn, such as status effects expiring or quests being completed.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item Game End
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game ends when a victory or defeat condition is met, such as all enemies being defeated or a player\textquotesingle{}s health reaching zero.
\item Players receive rewards or experience points based on their performance, and the game state is saved for future sessions. 
\end{DoxyItemize}
\end{DoxyEnumerate}
