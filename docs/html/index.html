Cardgame Project

Introduction

Welcome to the Cardgame Project, a turn-based, console-based card battle game written in C++. Players draw and play cards such as Strike, Armor Up, and Health Potion to defeat their opponent.

Gameplay Overview

  • Each player starts with a set amount of health.
  • Players draw cards from a deck each turn.
  • Cards have effects:
    • Strike deals damage to an opponent.
    • Armor Up increases the player’s armor.
    • Health Potion restores lost health.
  • Armor reduces incoming damage.
  • The game ends when one player's health reaches zero.
